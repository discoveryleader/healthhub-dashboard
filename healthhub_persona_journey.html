MR

Maria Reyes

The Coordinating Parent · Primary persona
38 years old Working parent 2 kids · ages 5 & 9 Laptop + tablet Suburban, dual-income household
“I just want to upload the vaccine form once and email a clean copy to the school without digging through three portals and a folder of paper.”

Bio & context

Maria manages doctor visits, school forms, and camp paperwork for the whole family. Between work and two kids on different schedules, she's the household's default record-keeper — the one who gets the text that says "the immunization form is due Friday."

Goals

  • Never miss a vaccine or form deadline
  • Produce required documents in minutes, not evenings
  • Keep each child's history accurate and in one place
  • Share only what a school or camp actually needs

Frustrations & pain points

  • Records scattered across 3 provider portals + paper
  • Re-typing the same immunization data every enrollment season
  • No single, printable source of truth on demand
  • Fear of missing an overdue vaccine

Needs from HealthHub

  • Upload-and-extract so she doesn't type records manually
  • At-a-glance alerts: overdue vaccines, upcoming visits
  • One-click formatted export & direct email
  • Per-child organization she can switch between fast

Tech comfort & behavior

Digital confidenceModerate–High
Time availableLow
Privacy sensitivityHigh

Uses web apps daily, mostly on laptop and tablet. Expects things to "just work" and distrusts anything that feels careless with her kids' data.

Success looks like

  • Uploads a vaccine record once → clean copy emailed to school in seconds
  • Opens the dashboard and instantly sees nothing is overdue
  • Trusts that shared records show only what she selected
DK

David Kim

The Co-Parent / Caregiver · Secondary persona
41 years old Co-parent · separate household Shared custody Phone + laptop Scoped access
“When the kids are with me and something comes up, I need to see their records — but only what I'm supposed to see.”

Bio & context

David is a co-parent in a separate household (the pattern also fits a grandparent or nanny with caregiving duties). He shares responsibility during his custody time and needs to view — and sometimes update — records without duplicating or conflicting with the account owner's record-keeping.

Goals

  • Stay informed on appointments and health status
  • Access the right child's records when caring for them
  • Avoid duplicate or contradictory records across homes

Frustrations & pain points

  • Being out of the loop on visits and changes
  • Not knowing which household holds the latest form
  • Unclear boundaries on what he can see or change

Needs from HealthHub

  • Scoped, permissioned access to specific children
  • Clear, visible boundaries on his rights
  • Shared visibility of appointments and alerts

Tech comfort & behavior

Digital confidenceModerate
Engagement frequencyPeriodic
Trust in boundariesNeeds reassurance

Comes in around custody transitions and events, not daily. Values clarity over power — he wants to know exactly what he can and can't do.

Success looks like

  • Accepts an invite and sees exactly the child(ren) he's granted
  • Checks upcoming appointments without calling the other parent
  • Understands his access can be adjusted or revoked at any time
